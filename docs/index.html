Immersive MP4 metadata

Tag video and audio
for immersive playback.

VR headsets and immersive apps read metadata inside your MP4, not the pixels or PCM samples. Spatial Media inspects and injects the tags for spherical video projection and ambisonic audio.

Without tags, immersive playback breaks.

A flat MP4 looks fine in QuickTime, but immersive players need explicit metadata. Spherical tags describe projection and stereo layout; spatial audio tags describe ambisonic channel order and normalization.

Spatial audio

First-order ambiX (ACN/SN3D). Writes the SA3D atom on compatible tracks.

One moov box. Video and audio atoms.

Metadata lives in the moov atom, not in encoded media. Spatial Media reads your file structure, shows existing tags, and writes spherical video (SV3D, ST3D, v1 XML) and spatial audio (SA3D) without re-encoding.

SpatialTag

Without tags, immersive playback breaks.

A flat MP4 looks fine in QuickTime, but immersive players need explicit metadata. Spherical tags describe projection and stereo layout; spatial audio tags describe ambisonic channel order and normalization.

No metadata

Flat video

360° content plays as a stretched rectangle instead of a sphere.

SV3D / XML

Immersive video

Player wraps frames on a sphere with correct stereo eye mapping.

SA3D

Spatial audio

Ambisonic tracks decode correctly instead of playing as flat stereo.

Video, VR180, and spatial audio.

360° equirectangular

Full sphere. Mono or stereo (side-by-side / over-under). v1 XML or v2 atoms.

VR180

Side-by-side frame, 180° forward view. Auto crop and bounds from resolution.

WYZX

Spatial audio

First-order ambiX (ACN/SN3D). Writes the SA3D atom on compatible tracks.

One moov box. Video and audio atoms.

Metadata lives in the moov atom, not in encoded media. SpatialTag reads your file structure, shows existing tags, and writes spherical video (SV3D, ST3D, v1 XML) and spatial audio (SA3D) without re-encoding.
